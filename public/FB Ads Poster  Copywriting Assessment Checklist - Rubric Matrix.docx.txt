FB Ads Poster & Copywriting Assessment Checklist + Rubric Matrix
From a Senior Facebook Ads Marketing Manager — use this as the mandatory pre-launch gate. If the final weighted score is below baseline, DO NOT LAUNCH PAID ADS (it will waste ad spend).
________________


How to use this checklist (quick)
1. Score each main element (1–4) against the rubric descriptors.
2. Compute Weighted Score = (Score ÷ 4) × Element Weight.
3. Sum all weighted scores → Total /100.
4. Compare to baseline: Prospecting / cold audience baseline = 85; Retargeting / warm audience baseline = 75. If below baseline → HOLD and fix issues noted.
________________


Weights (total = 100)
* Attention (visual & headline): 20
* Interest (opening lines, relevance): 15
* Desire (benefits, PAS — pain & agitate): 15
* Offer (value proposition, clarity, urgency): 15
* Credential (social proof, trust signals): 10
* Action (CTA clarity & friction): 10
* Compliance & Technical (policy, creative specs, tracking): 15
________________


Rubric matrix — detailed checklist
1) ATTENTION — Visual & Headline (Weight 20)
Scoring focuses on whether the creative stops the scroll and clearly signals what the ad is about.
Sub-criteria (score 1–4 applies to overall Attention):
* Visual Impact

   * 4 — Powerful single focal point, strong contrast, human presence or striking iconography, on-brand.
   * 3 — Clear focal point, decent composition; may need slight improvement in visual hierarchy.
   * 2 — Busy or bland; focal point unclear.
   * 1 — Confusing or low-quality image; will not stop the scroll.
   * Headline Hook (≤8 words ideal)

      * 4 — Instant benefit or provocative question; tailored to audience.
      * 3 — Benefit but weak specificity.
      * 2 — Vague statement; passive.
      * 1 — No headline or irrelevant tagline.
      * Readability (mobile-first)

         * 4 — Text large, legible on mobile, clear contrast.
         * 3 — Readable but borderline on small screens.
         * 2 — Hard to read on mobile.
         * 1 — Illegible on mobile.
Actionable fail signs: image noisy, headline unrelated to offer, text too small.
________________


2) INTEREST — Opening Copy & Relevance (Weight 15)
Does copy create immediate relevance and emotional engagement in the first 1–2 lines?
         * Audience Relevance

            * 4 — Opens with audience-specific pain or situation.
            * 3 — Generic relevance; could be more audience-specific.
            * 2 — Weak audience fit.
            * 1 — Off-target.
            * Hook Continuation (first two lines)

               * 4 — Expands headline with curiosity or a strong promise.
               * 3 — Continues the headline but lacks punch.
               * 2 — Slow start; loses attention.
               * 1 — No hook.
               * PAS — Pain identification (P)

                  * 4 — Clearly states the real, specific pain the audience feels.
                  * 3 — Pain is present but generic.
                  * 2 — Pain unclear.
                  * 1 — No pain mentioned.
________________


3) DESIRE — Benefits, Emotional Triggers, PAS Agitation (Weight 15)
Builds desire by agitating the pain and presenting benefit-driven outcomes.
                  * Agitate the Pain (A in PAS)

                     * 4 — Amplifies consequences if pain continues; emotionally resonant.
                     * 3 — Some agitation but could be stronger.
                     * 2 — Minimal agitation.
                     * 1 — Skips agitation.
                     * Benefit Statements (not features)

                        * 4 — Clear tangible benefits (time, money, status, ease), measurable when possible.
                        * 3 — Benefits present but mixed with features.
                        * 2 — Mostly features.
                        * 1 — No benefits.
                        * Transformation / Desired Outcome

                           * 4 — Paints a believable ‘after’ state; short-term + long-term outcomes.
                           * 3 — Vague transformation.
                           * 2 — Weak future-state description.
                           * 1 — None.
________________


4) OFFER — Clarity, Value, Urgency, PAS Solution (Weight 15)
Is the offer crystal clear and valuable? Does it solve the pain (S in PAS)?
                           * Offer Clarity

                              * 4 — Exactly what user gets, price or free, period, duration, next step.
                              * 3 — Mostly clear but missing one small detail.
                              * 2 — Confusing offer elements.
                              * 1 — Offer unclear or hidden.
                              * Value Proposition & ROI

                                 * 4 — Explicit value (e.g., save X, earn Y, reduce Z) or clear free-value.
                                 * 3 — Value implied but not quantified.
                                 * 2 — Weak value; no reason to act.
                                 * 1 — No value shown.
                                 * Urgency / Scarcity / CTA trigger

                                    * 4 — Honest urgency or scarcity (limited seats, deadline) with expiry.
                                    * 3 — Mild urgency, weak proof.
                                    * 2 — No urgency.
                                    * 1 — Fake urgency or misleading claim.
                                    * PAS — Solution & Steps (S)

                                       * 4 — Clear, simple steps to solve pain; confidence in outcome.
                                       * 3 — Solution present but lacks step clarity.
                                       * 2 — Solution vague.
                                       * 1 — No solution described.
________________


5) CREDENTIAL — Trust, Social Proof, Guarantees (Weight 10)
Signals that reduce perceived risk.
                                       * Social Proof (testimonials, logos, numbers)

                                          * 4 — Relevant testimonial, case stat, or trusted client logos visible & credible.
                                          * 3 — Generic testimonial or number; could be more specific.
                                          * 2 — Minimal proof.
                                          * 1 — No proof.
                                          * Guarantees / Risk Reducers

                                             * 4 — Money-back, trial, or strong guarantee stated.
                                             * 3 — Implicit risk reducer but not explicit.
                                             * 2 — Weak/no guarantees.
                                             * 1 — No risk reducer.
                                             * Authority Signals (years, awards, certs)

                                                * 4 — Clean, concise authority indicators included.
                                                * 3 — Some authority but cluttered.
                                                * 2 — Weak authority.
                                                * 1 — None.
________________


6) ACTION — CTA & Conversion Friction (Weight 10)
Is the next step obvious and easy?
                                                * CTA Clarity

                                                   * 4 — Direct command: “Book 15-min consult”, “Get 30% off now”, “Download guide” — single clear button/line.
                                                   * 3 — CTA present but multiple CTAs causing confusion.
                                                   * 2 — CTA vague (“Learn more”).
                                                   * 1 — No CTA.
                                                   * Friction (forms, steps, link correctness)

                                                      * 4 — 1-click or single-field lead form; link verified; fast-loading landing page.
                                                      * 3 — Slight friction (multi-field) but acceptable.
                                                      * 2 — Several steps; long form.
                                                      * 1 — Broken link or landing mismatch.
                                                      * CTA Visual Prominence

                                                         * 4 — CTA stands out visually (button shape, size) and repeated in copy.
                                                         * 3 — Present but not prominent.
                                                         * 2 — Hard to spot.
                                                         * 1 — Missing.
________________


7) COMPLIANCE & TECHNICAL — Policy, Specs, Tracking (Weight 15)
Ads must pass platform rules and technical specs.
                                                         * FB Policy Compliance (no prohibited content, no misleading claims)

                                                            * 4 — Fully compliant; legal, no exaggerated health/financial claims without substantiation.
                                                            * 3 — Minor wording risk; should be rephrased.
                                                            * 2 — Potential policy flag; likely to be disapproved.
                                                            * 1 — Non-compliant content.
                                                            * Creative Specs & File Quality

                                                               * 4 — Correct sizes, safe zones, <3MB (if needed), high resolution, correct aspect ratio, font embedding ok.
                                                               * 3 — Minor tech issue (small crop).
                                                               * 2 — Multiple spec violations.
                                                               * 1 — Unusable file.
                                                               * Tracking & Pixel

                                                                  * 4 — Pixel events set, UTM tags present, landing page has tracking + fast TTFB.
                                                                  * 3 — Basic tracking present; e.g., only landing page tag.
                                                                  * 2 — No tracking or broken events.
                                                                  * 1 — Tracking will not record conversions.
                                                                  * Text-to-Image Ratio & Read Restrictions (best practice)

                                                                     * 4 — Minimal text overlay; copy primarily in primary text.
                                                                     * 3 — Moderate text; likely fine.
                                                                     * 2 — Heavy text that reduces reach.
                                                                     * 1 — Too much text or text as image-only.

Quick pre-launch QA (5 steps every ad MUST pass)
                                                                     1. One-line test: Can someone explain the ad's offer in one sentence within 3 seconds?
                                                                     2. Mobile preview: Check creative, headline, first two lines on a small screen.
                                                                     3. Landing match: Click ad → landing page message and offer match 1:1.
                                                                     4. Tracking check: Pixel fires and UTM recorded. Test a dummy conversion.
                                                                     5. Policy scan: No exaggerated health/financial claims; no restricted content; images of minors, before/after, or risky content flagged.
________________


Decision rule (explicit)
                                                                     * If Total Score < Baseline → HOLD (no paid spend).
                                                                     * If Total Score ≥ Baseline → LAUNCH (but monitor first 24–72 hours closely).
Recommended baselines (strict): Prospecting = 85, Retargeting = 75. Team lead may lower retargeting to 70 for urgent tests with small budgets, but only with documented rationale.
________________


If the ad fails: Root-cause feedback snippets (copy-paste to designer/sales exec)
                                                                     * Attention fail: “Visual doesn’t stop the scroll. Replace with single focal image (happy customer / strong product shot). Increase headline contrast and shorten to 6–8 words. Mobile preview required.”
                                                                     * Interest fail: “Opening lines aren’t audience-specific. Add a one-line pain statement referencing [audience job/title/industry].”
                                                                     * Desire fail: “Benefits are feature-heavy. Rewrite to show 2–3 measurable benefits (time saved, % increase, money saved). Add an emotional sentence that shows the cost of inaction.”
                                                                     * Offer fail: “Offer unclear. State exactly what they get, the price or free value, and the expiry. Add 3-step promise: what → how → guarantee.”
                                                                     * Credential fail: “Add one short testimonial or client logo and a clear guarantee (7-day refund / free trial). Replace generic claims with customer proof.”
                                                                     * Action fail: “Make CTA a single clear action; reduce form fields to 2 or use instant form.”
                                                                     * Compliance fail: “Reword the claim to a substantiated statement; remove sensitive imagery. Verify pixel & UTM.”
