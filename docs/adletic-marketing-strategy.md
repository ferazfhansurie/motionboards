# ADletic Agency — Marketing Strategy Research

Research on how to market **ADletic Agency** (Firaz's Malaysian-localised video ad
production agency, built on top of **MotionBoards**) to win more clients. This is
about growing the agency's own client base — distinct from campaign work ADletic
does *for* clients like FatHopes Energy or ESP Wrapz.

Sources consulted: see [Sources](#sources) at the bottom.

---

## 1. Where ADletic already stands (starting point)

- **Proven format, not a pitch deck.** ADletic already has a repeatable, locked
  video formula (kinetic-typographic event recaps, Gen-Z "funny but kinda
  serious" branded shorts, PUSH Life mockumentary) and live client work
  (FatHopes UCO recruitment + community collection, ESP Wrapz car detailing).
  That's real portfolio, not theory.
- **A genuine differentiator most agencies don't have:** ADletic's founder
  *builds the AI production tool the agency runs on* (MotionBoards). Almost no
  competitor can say "we didn't just adopt AI video tools, we engineer one."
  That's the single strongest positioning wedge available and should anchor
  everything below.
- **Malaysian-market fluency**: KL Manglish / ~80% Bahasa Melayu copy, local
  cultural references (JDT jerseys, Kelantan dialect, kampung/mall roadshows).
  This is a moat against generic/regional agencies and international AI-video
  shops that can't localise voice.

## 2. Positioning (what to lead with)

> "AI-native, Malaysia-fluent video ads — built by an agency that engineers its
> own production tools."

Three pillars to repeat everywhere (bio, deck, landing page, pitch call):
1. **Speed & cost**: AI-assisted production (Seedance/Nano Banana/Veo via
   MotionBoards) means turnaround in days not weeks, at SME-friendly rates —
   without the "cheap AI slop" look, because real photos anchor every character.
2. **Cultural fluency**: Manglish/BM copy, local dialects, local settings —
   competitors doing generic English AI-ad templates cannot match this for
   Malaysian SMEs.
3. **Full-funnel, not just pretty video**: recap videos, recruitment creative,
   Meta Ads media buying and lead-quality analysis (CPL, CTR, kill/scale
   decisions) all under one roof — the FatHopes engagement proves this end-to-
   end capability, not just "we made a video."

## 3. Target niches (specialize, don't generalize)

Broad "we do video for anyone" is the weakest possible pitch. Research
consistently shows niche-specialized agencies win because they can point to a
directly comparable case study. Pick 2-3 verticals where ADletic already has
proof or adjacent proof:

- **Recruitment / field-agent driven businesses** (FatHopes PUSH is exactly
  this: recruiting "usahawan hijau" collectors) — a repeatable niche of
  Malaysian SMEs that need constant recruitment-ad creative (F&B franchise
  agents, insurance agents, direct-sales, gig-economy fleets).
- **Community/CSR-driven brands** running recurring local events (roadshows,
  collection drives, campus activations) that need recap content — the
  FatHopes Cahaya Alam / FELCRA recap format is a packaged, repeatable
  product ("Event Recap Package: RMX, delivered in 48h").
  Automotive/lifestyle SME services (ESP Wrapz-style: detailing, grooming,
  home services) — short-form Gen-Z-style Reels/TikTok ads for local service
  businesses, a very large underserved SME segment in Malaysia.

Pick the niche(s) with the clearest existing before/after numbers (FatHopes'
RM0.99 CPL Kelantan result is a genuinely strong stat) and build outward from
there rather than trying to serve everyone at once.

## 4. The content/case-study engine (primary lead-gen channel)

Per current research, **70%+ of B2B buyers review a case study before ever
contacting a provider**, and agencies with high referral rates build "sticky"
case-study-led trust rather than cold outreach alone. ADletic should treat its
own client work as the marketing:

- **Publish a real case study per completed campaign**: FatHopes PUSH
  recruitment (CPL/CTR numbers, Kelantan-targeting insight, kill/scale
  decisions), FatHopes event recaps (50KG / 115KG, deals closed with Koperasi
  FELCRA & Bomba Masjid Tanah), ESP Wrapz. Structure each as: **Challenge →
  Insight → Creative Execution → Result (numbers)** — this is the exact
  structure top-performing agency case studies use.
- **Behind-the-scenes / "how we built this" content**: since ADletic's edge is
  building its own AI tool, short videos/posts showing the MotionBoards
  workflow (prompt → Seedance/Nano Banana output → final ad) double as both
  product marketing for MotionBoards and credibility marketing for ADletic.
  This is a two-for-one content engine most competitors structurally can't
  replicate.
- **Repurpose across formats**: one case study → LinkedIn post (for the
  B2B/SME-owner audience), a TikTok/Reel breakdown (process + result),
  and a one-pager PDF/WhatsApp-shareable card for direct outreach.

## 5. Channel plan

| Channel | Role | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **WhatsApp direct outreach + shareable case-study cards** | Highest-intent, lowest-cost, culturally native for Malaysian SME decision-makers | Mirrors the "click-to-WhatsApp" tactic research flags as a top Malaysian SME lead-gen channel; send the 1-pager, not a cold pitch |
| **Meta Ads (own account, lead-gen objective)** | Paid client acquisition, proven by ADletic's own FatHopes results | Apply the same lead-objective-over-traffic, kill/scale discipline ADletic already uses for FatHopes to its own client-acquisition campaigns |
| **LinkedIn / Google Business Profile + local SEO** | Inbound credibility + "video ads Malaysia" / "[niche] ad agency Malaysia" search capture | GBP + local SEO is called out repeatedly as high-converting, low-cost for Malaysian SMEs; low effort, long payoff — worth 1-2 hrs/month |
| **Referral program with existing clients** | Compounding, near-zero CAC | Formalize: ask FatHopes/ESP Wrapz for 1 warm intro per quarter in exchange for a discount/added deliverable; referral-led agencies see the highest retention in the research |
| **Firaz's personal TikTok (@faeezree)** | Indirect brand halo, not a hard sell | Keep it raw/unscripted per existing house rule — do not turn it into an ADletic ad channel, but a bio link + "I run ADletic Agency" framing lets curious viewers self-select into DMs |

## 6. Packaging (make it easy to buy)

Rather than bespoke-quote-everything, package the proven formats as named
products with a price anchor, mirroring "Event Recap Package" logic already
implicit in the recap formula:
- **Event Recap Package** (kinetic-typo recap from real event photos, 48h turnaround)
- **Recruitment Creative Sprint** (poster + video set for field-agent recruiting, Meta-ready)
- **SME Short-Form Starter** (3-video Reels/TikTok pack, Gen-Z funny-but-serious formula)
- **Always-on Ads Management** (creative + media buying + CPL/CTR reporting, the FatHopes model)

Clear packages shorten the sales cycle and make WhatsApp outreach and
referrals easy to convert without a custom proposal each time.

## 7. 90-day action plan

1. **Weeks 1-2**: Write and publish 2 case studies (FatHopes PUSH CPL result;
   FatHopes event recap) in the Challenge→Insight→Execution→Result structure.
   Turn each into a WhatsApp-shareable one-pager.
2. **Weeks 1-4**: Set up Google Business Profile for ADletic Agency; optimise
   for "[service] ad agency Malaysia" / "video ads for SME Malaysia".
3. **Weeks 2-6**: Launch a small owned Meta lead-gen campaign for ADletic
   itself, applying the same lead-objective + Kelantan-style local targeting
   discipline used for FatHopes. Track CPL from day 1.
4. **Ongoing**: 1 "behind the MotionBoards workflow" post/video per week —
   this is cheap to produce (screen-record actual production sessions) and
   is the differentiator content competitors can't copy.
5. **End of quarter**: Ask each active client (FatHopes, ESP Wrapz, etc.) for
   one warm referral; formalize a small referral incentive.
6. **Ongoing**: Track and report simple KPIs monthly (below) so the agency's
   own funnel gets the same rigor ADletic applies to client accounts.

## 8. KPIs to track for ADletic's own funnel

- Inbound leads by channel (WhatsApp, Meta, GBP/organic search, referral)
- Cost per lead on the owned Meta campaign (compare against the FatHopes
  benchmark of ~RM0.99-RM3.15 CPL as a sanity check)
- Case-study page views / one-pager shares → meeting conversion rate
- Referral rate (% of new clients from existing-client referrals)
- Package take-rate (which named package converts best, to double down on)
