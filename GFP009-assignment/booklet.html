01 · The problem

We cram. Then we forget.

Lectures dump everything at once. Working memory overloads.
Without review, we forget most of it within days (Ebbinghaus, 1885).
So we cram. High stress, and it fades right after.
50-80%forgotten within days
2
02 · The solution

Meet MicroMentor.

MicroMentor turns your lecture notes into short, personalised lessons, with quizzes and spaced reminders so it sticks.
3 to 5 minute lessons. One idea at a time, on your phone.
Smart review. Reminds you right before you would forget.
Learn together. A class leaderboard and a Q and A wall.
3
03 · The model

Built on AMPLIFY

A microlearning model by Nurul Fitriah Alias (UM, 2024). Its seven components became our seven design decisions.
1Clear learning objectivesOne goal per lesson
2Personalised contentCourse and weak spots
3Continuous feedbackInstant, spaced review
4Efficient content deliveryShort, mobile, offline
5Interactive experiencesTap, drag, scenarios
6Social interactionLeaderboard, Q and A
7Seamless curriculum integrationMapped to syllabus

05 · How we designed it

Aina's story in six frames

1

The problem

120 slides the night before her exam. Panic.

2

Discovery

She opens MicroMentor. Three short lessons today.

3

Learning

A three minute lesson, one goal, tap to answer.

4

Feedback

Instant tick and a reason. It returns tomorrow.

5

Together

She checks the leaderboard and asks on the wall.

6

Outcome

Exam week. Calm, confident, and she remembers.

We tested, then changed
No reason given for a wrong answer→Added a one line why
Making a lesson took too long→Six steps down to three
Leaderboard felt stressful→Opt in and class only
