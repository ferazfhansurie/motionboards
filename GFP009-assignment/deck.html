The problem
We cram. Then we forget.
Lectures dump everything at once. Working memory overloads.
Without review, we forget most of it within days (Ebbinghaus, 1885).
So we cram. High stress, and it fades right after.

Our solution
MicroMentor turns your lecture notes into short, personalised lessons, with quizzes and spaced reminders so it sticks.
3 to 5 min lessons. One idea at a time, on your phone.
Smart review. Reminds you right before you would forget.
Learn together. A class leaderboard and a Q and A wall.

Applying the AMPLIFY model
AMPLIFY, applied to MicroMentor
AMPLIFY is a microlearning model by Nurul Fitriah Alias (UM, 2024). Seven components, seven design decisions.
1Clear learning objectivesOne goal per lesson.
2Personalised contentAdapts to your course and weak spots.
3Continuous feedbackInstant answers, spaced reminders.
4Efficient content delivery3 to 5 minutes, mobile, offline.
5Interactive experiencesTap, drag, scenario questions.
6Social interactionClass leaderboard and Q and A wall.
7Seamless curriculum integrationMapped to your syllabus.

Testing & evaluation
We tested with peers, then changed four things
WHAT USERS SAID
WHAT WE CHANGED
Feedback did not say why an answer was wrong.
→
Added a one line explanation to every result.
Making a lesson took too many steps.
→
Cut the flow from six steps to three.
The leaderboard felt stressful for some.
→
Made it opt in and class only.
People wanted a language choice.
→
Added a Bahasa Melayu and English toggle.
