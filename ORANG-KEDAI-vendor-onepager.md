# ORANG KEDAI — Vendor Shoot One-Pager

A reusable flow for the vendor-spotlight series. Fill in the top, follow the steps, don't miss anything.
**Tone:** laid-back. The vendor is the star, you barely talk. One dry line about the oil income — in *their* words, never a pitch. Other vendors watch and quietly want their stall filmed too.

## 1. BEFORE YOU GO
- [ ] Vendor agrees to be filmed, posted, and named (verbal or quick written OK)
- [ ] Confirm they actually sell oil to FatHopes — so the income bit is real
- [ ] Pick a **quiet hour** (so he can talk) + catch a **busy moment** (for B-roll)
- [ ] Bring: phone/cam (vertical 9:16), clip/lav mic, small light for night, spare battery, lens cloth
- [ ] Order and eat the dish yourself — you'll want it on camera fresh

---

## 2. WHAT TO FILM (checklist)
**The craft**
- [ ] Wok/fryer in action — fire, pour, scrape, his signature move
- [ ] Hands, close up, working
- [ ] The food — the money shot, sizzling / plating / bungkus

**The place**
- [ ] Wide of the stall — day AND night with lights on
- [ ] The sign, menu, handwritten notes, the "tutup hari ___" sign
- [ ] The corner, the street, the surroundings

**The people**
- [ ] Regulars eating, the queue
- [ ] A regular who's come for years (grab a quick word if they're willing)
- [ ] His face while working — not posed

**The oil**
- [ ] The used-oil tin in the back
- [ ] Him near the tin for the income beat

**For the quiet ending**
- [ ] A few seconds of "nothing" — him wiping down, waiting, lights on, dark around

## 3. THE INTERVIEW (ask casually, in KL Malay — let him ramble, don't rush)

**A. His story**
- Berapa lama dah buka kedai ni?
- Macam mana mula-mula? Sapa ajar?
- Dulu buat kerja apa sebelum ni?
- Kenapa pilih jual benda ni?
- Tempat ni je ke, atau pernah pindah-pindah?

**B. His way / the craft**
- Apa rahsia [makanan dia]? (tanya je, walaupun mesti dia tak bagitau)
- Sehari guna berapa kilo? Buat berapa plate/bungkus?
- Ada benda kau memang refuse nak buat? (tak nak tambah ___, tak nak buat banyak sekali gus, dsb)
- Apa orang selalu salah faham pasal kerja ni?
- Kalau hujan / pasar sepi, macam mana?

**C. Character / opinions (the spicy bits)**
- Apa yang buat kau geram pasal pelanggan?
- Kedai sebelah / seberang tu — kawan ke lawan? (laugh)
- Apa yang dah berubah sejak kau mula dulu?
- Pernah rasa nak tutup terus tak? Bila?
- Kalau anak nak ambil alih, kau bagi tak?

**D. His people**
- Pelanggan paling lama — berapa tahun dah datang?
- Ada pelanggan yang dah jadi macam keluarga?
- Cerita pelanggan paling pelik atau paling kelakar?
- Orang datang dari jauh tak semata-mata nak makan sini?

**E. The oil + FatHopes (keep it ONE short, dry exchange — don't push)**
- Minyak lama ni dulu kau buat apa?
- Sejak bila FatHopes ambil?
- Macam mana rasa dapat duit dari benda yang dulu kau buang je?
- Sebulan lebih kurang dapat berapa? (kalau dia selesa cakap)
- Kau cadang tak kat kedai lain buat sama?
> The gold line is the "dulu buang, sekarang dapat duit" energy. His shrug does the selling.

**F. Closing**
- Bila masa terbaik orang datang?
- Apa kau nak orang ingat pasal kedai ni?
- Lepas ni ada plan apa? Buka lagi satu? Nak rehat?

## 4. AUDIO + TECHNICAL (don't skip)
- Vertical 9:16
- Clip mic on the vendor — clean audio matters more than the picture
- Record ambient separately (wok, street, frying) for the edit
- Natural light, or one soft light at night
- Let silences sit. Don't fill them. Don't over-ask.
- Talk to him like a regular customer, not an interviewer

---

## 5. THE EDIT (beat order)
1. Drop straight into the craft — no intro, ambient only (0–3s)
2. One question → let him talk
3. Texture: regulars, hands, the sign (B-roll over his voice)
4. The one oil/income exchange — dry, mid-video, NO graphic, NO "learn more"
5. Quiet ending — wide, lights on, the "nothing" shot
6. Name card: vendor name, stall, location, hours
7. Last small line: **"Kedai kau pun ada cerita? Bagitau kami."**

---

## 6. POSTING
- Caption: one line, his voice or about him + the location. No hashtag spam.
- Tag / name the area so locals share it.
- Comment bait: "Korang tau kedai macam ni? Tag." / "Area mana kita pergi next?"
- Pin a comment on how to get featured (soft, no pressure)
- Keep it consistent: same title card + same ending line every episode

---

- [ ] Ask before filming other customers' faces (or keep them background)
