### CRITICAL composition rule (this is what the first attempt missed)

The cars must read as **stopped at a red light**, not staged in the
middle of an empty intersection. The previous generation placed the cars
in front of the traffic light with no stop line visible. The corrected
prompt below explicitly puts the cars **behind** a painted white stop
line and zebra crosswalk, with the traffic light's pole / gantry visibly
positioned AT the cars' intersection rather than far in the background.

```

directly above them), red signal illuminated and clearly visible. The
```
