# Visual Style Guide

## Core Art Direction

The series uses a mixed-reality style:

- Real photographed or filmed Malaysian environments.
- 2D animated superhero characters composited into the scene.
- Cartoon physics for characters, real physics for objects unless exaggerated for jokes.
- Clean anime/comic character linework.
- Real-world background lighting should influence the character shading.

Think: playful mixed-media animated characters living inside ordinary real locations, with the comedy coming from that contrast.

Do not describe the art style as copying any specific existing show. Use this direction instead:

> mixed-media animated comedy, 2D comic/anime heroes composited into real Malaysian photo/video backgrounds, expressive cartoon acting, realistic environment textures, playful scale contrast, clean bold silhouettes

## World Texture

Use real-feeling locations:

- FatHopes depot
- tanker parking bay
- restaurant back alley
- mamak kitchen
- pasar malam
- food court loading area
- wet market
- school or hospital recycling booth
- drains and grease traps
- apartment shoplots
- petrol station rest stop

## Character Rendering Rules

- Bold black/near-black outlines.
- Flat comic colors with controlled shadow blocks.
- Expressive eyes and mouth shapes.
- Strong silhouettes, recognisable even at phone-screen size.
- Slightly exaggerated poses.
- Do not over-render skin or fabric; keep characters readable.
- Keep hero outfits consistent across episodes.

## Background Rules

- Backgrounds should be real or real-looking.
- Avoid glossy futuristic cityscapes unless it is a fantasy gag.
- The world should feel practical: plastic drums, stained floors, signage, cones, pipes, gloves, app screens, tanker hoses.

## Compositing Rules

- Match character feet to the ground plane.
- Add soft contact shadows under characters.
- Add slight rim light if background is bright.
- Characters may be more saturated than the environment, but not neon.
- If a character touches a real object, either animate the object or cut around it carefully.

## Camera Language

- Handheld phone documentary for comedy scenes.
- Fast push-ins for panic or discovery.
- Static wide shots for awkward pauses.
- Hero low-angle shots only for intentionally dramatic moments.
- Reaction close-ups are important.

## Palette

Primary:

- FatHopes green
- deep teal
- black
- white
- grey industrial neutrals

Accents:

- bright oil yellow
- hazard orange
- app-screen cyan
- reflective safety silver

Avoid:

- one-note neon green everywhere
- generic superhero red/blue
- overly glossy futuristic purple/blue gradients

## Consistency Checklist

Before approving any visual:

- Does the hero outfit match their character sheet?
- Does the background feel like real Malaysia?
- Is the UCO object specific and visible?
- Does the image read clearly on a phone?
- Is the character grounded with shadow/contact?
- Is the FatHopes world practical, not fantasy-generic?
