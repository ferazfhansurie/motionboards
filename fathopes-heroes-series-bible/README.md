# FatHopes Heroes Animated Series Bible

This folder keeps the rules, designs, language, and story planning for the FatHopes Energy superhero animated series.

Use it as the source of truth before writing scripts, generating art, briefing animators, or making episode prompts.

## Core Idea

FatHopes Energy heroes protect Malaysia from the quiet everyday chaos of waste: used cooking oil poured into drains, restaurants ignoring collection, illegal resale, route delays, contaminated barrels, and people not realising that old oil can become real green value.

The comedy comes from treating small UCO problems like full superhero emergencies.

Example:

> A mamak uncle says, "Ala sikit je minyak masuk longkang."
>
> The heroes react like he just opened a portal to disaster.

## Series Format

- Short-form animated episodes, 45 seconds to 3 minutes.
- Real Malaysian environments with 2D animated heroes composited on top.
- Manglish dialogue, with each character having a different English/Malay balance.
- Comedy first, mission second, education underneath.
- Every episode should leave the audience understanding one FatHopes idea:
  - used cooking oil has value
  - do not pour oil into drains
  - vendors can request collection
  - recycling supports cleaner fuel and cleaner communities
  - small daily habits can become big climate action

## Current Cast

- Dr.UP - confident talker / public-facing hero.
- Sparron - green-glasses girl / sharp strategist and tech-energy hero.
- Kit - hard worker / field operator who does the heavy lifting.
- Glinciro - hat-wearing villain / charming anti-recycler troublemaker.

Names can be adjusted later, but do not rename characters casually across prompts. Consistent naming is how you keep designs consistent.

## Series Growth Rule

Every main character needs a season arc, not just a fixed gimmick:

- Dr.UP learns confidence is stronger when he listens.
- Sparron learns strategy must connect with real people.
- Kit learns hard work does not mean carrying everything alone.
- Glinciro slowly reveals why he fights the system and can become more complex than a simple villain.
