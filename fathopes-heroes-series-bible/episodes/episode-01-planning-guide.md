# Episode 1 Planning Guide

## Working Title

The Oil That Was Almost Gone

## Episode Goal

Introduce the FatHopes heroes, the mixed-reality world, and the core idea:

> Used cooking oil is not trash. If handled properly, it has value and can be collected.

## Why This Should Be Episode 1

Episode 1 should not start with lore, origin stories, or a full villain.

Start with one simple, everyday problem: a food vendor is about to pour used cooking oil into a drain because they think it is useless.

That instantly explains the world:

- real Malaysian food business
- used cooking oil problem
- heroes appear
- comedy from overreaction
- FatHopes solution

## Core Premise

At the back of a busy mamak/warung, a vendor carries a container of used cooking oil toward the drain.

Kit spots it like a field emergency because he knows he will be the one cleaning the mess later.
Dr.UP arrives with confident hero energy.
Sparron scans the oil and calls it "recoverable material."
Glinciro appears as the villain, trying to convince the vendor that dumping it is faster.

The vendor asks the real question:

> "So... ada duit ke?"

That is the moment the episode becomes practical.

## Main Message

Do not pour used cooking oil into drains.
Store it properly and request FatHopes collection.

## Episode Structure

### 0-3 Seconds: Hook

Visual:
A hand tilts a container of used oil toward a drain.

Sound:
Overdramatic superhero alarm.

Kit:
"JANGAN."

Cut to silence. Vendor freezes.

### 3-10 Seconds: The Ordinary Problem

Vendor:
"Apa benda? Minyak lama je."

Kit:
"Minyak lama? Bro, itu future value."

Dr.UP enters dramatically in the back alley.

Dr.UP:
"Boss, this one still has value if you keep it clean."

He notices a tiny oily puddle, pauses, and steps around it before continuing.

### 10-25 Seconds: Team Clash

Sparron scans the oil.

Sparron:
"Quality acceptable. Minor food particles. No water contamination."

Vendor:
"English sikit-sikit boleh?"

Kit:
"Maksud dia, minyak ni masih boleh collect."

Kit points at the drain while still holding the heavy container.

Kit:
"Longkang bukan tempat buang minyak. Nanti mampat, busuk, semua orang pening."

### 25-45 Seconds: The FatHopes Solution

Kit shows phone/app.

Kit:
"Simpan dalam tong, jangan campur air, then request collection."

Vendor:
"Korang datang ambil?"

Kit:
"Yes. FatHopes datang collect."

Vendor:
"So... ada duit ke?"

Everyone turns to camera.

Kit:
"Finally, soalan yang betul."

### 45-60 Seconds: Button Ending

Dr.UP:
"From waste to value."

Sparron:
"And potentially into cleaner fuel pathways."

Vendor:
"Okay okay, as long jangan panjang sangat."

Kit:
"Download app. Three taps je."

Final visual:
The drain is safe. The oil container has a "FatHopes collection" label. Kit finally puts the heavy tong down.

Kit:
"Tong saved. Mission complete."

## Episode 1 Character Jobs

- Kit brings the urgency, labour, and field rule.
- Sparron brings the technical diagnosis.
- Dr.UP brings confident public explanation.
- Glinciro brings villain temptation and the shortcut.
- Vendor keeps everyone practical.

## Location

Best:

- real mamak/warung back alley
- visible drain
- oil container
- kitchen or fryer nearby

Alternative:

- FatHopes depot reenactment
- food court loading area
- staged restaurant backdoor

## Props

- used cooking oil container
- blue barrel or tong
- funnel
- gloves
- phone with app screen
- drain
- small "FatHopes collection" label

## What To Avoid

- Do not explain SAF in detail in Episode 1.
- Do not introduce too many villains.
- Do not start with a long origin monologue.
- Do not make the vendor dumb.
- Do not make the CTA feel like a hard ad until the end.

## Stronger Ending Options

Option A:

Vendor asks:
"Kalau minyak banyak, kopi belanja tak?"

Kit:
"Minyak dulu. Kopi later."

Option B:

Dr.UP says:
"The planet thanks you."

Vendor:
"Planet bayar cash ke?"

Kit:
"FatHopes handles that part."

Option C:

Sparron says:
"We prevented drainage contamination."

Kit:
"Translation: longkang tak nangis hari ni."

## Episode 1 Writing Checklist

Before writing the script, answer:

- What exact vendor type is this? Mamak, ayam goreng, nasi campur, burger, pasar malam?
- Where is the drain?
- Why is the vendor about to throw the oil?
- What does Kit physically have to deal with?
- What does Sparron over-explain?
- What does Dr.UP confidently say?
- What bad shortcut does Glinciro offer?
- What is the last joke?
- What is the CTA?

## First Draft Script Template

Scene:
Real Malaysian food-business back alley. Afternoon. Busy kitchen noise.

Hook:
[Vendor walks toward drain with used oil.]

Kit:
"JANGAN."

Vendor:
"Eh siapa pulak ni?"

Then write the scene in short beats. Keep each line under 10 words when possible.
