# Episode 1 Careful Plan

## Working Title

Kacau Sikit

## Core Episode Shape

Episode 1 has two parts:

1. Day 1:
   A vendor almost pours used cooking oil into the longkang. Kit stops him with "JANGAN." The heroes explain just enough for the vendor to save the oil properly.

2. Another day:
   The vendor's container is full. He is about to call FatHopes for collection. Before he can finish, Glinciro appears from the dark. He does not steal the oil. He sabotages it.

This introduces the real enemy logic of G.O.M.:

> They do not always need to take the resource. Sometimes they only need to ruin it, confuse people, and push them back into careless disposal.

## The Aha

The click should be:

> Saving oil is only half the fight. Keeping it clean from sabotage is what protects its value.

Dr.UP version:

"He did not want the oil. He wanted us to lose the oil."

Kit version:

"Dia tak curi. Dia kacau sampai benda ni jadi masalah balik."

## Main Message

Do not pour used cooking oil into the longkang.
Save it clean, keep it covered, do not mix water or rubbish, and request FatHopes collection.

## G.O.M. Threat

Geng Orang Minyak spreads chaos through:

- careless disposal
- contaminated oil
- fake shortcuts
- low awareness
- vendors believing proper collection is troublesome

Glinciro is the first face of that threat.

## Glinciro's Motive

Glinciro is not sabotaging the tong just because he enjoys mess.

He is protecting G.O.M.'s dirty economy.

When a vendor stores oil cleanly and calls FatHopes, that oil becomes traceable value. It leaves the shadow market, avoids the drain, and becomes part of a cleaner system.

That is bad for G.O.M.

So Glinciro attacks the vendor's trust.

His motive in Episode 1:

> Make the vendor feel that saving oil properly is still troublesome, dangerous, and not worth the effort.

If the vendor gives up after one bad experience, G.O.M. wins. The oil goes back to the longkang, mixed containers, shady buyers, and pollution.

Glinciro's quiet logic:

"Satu vendor hilang percaya, sepuluh tong balik longkang."

## Glinciro's Episode Move

Glinciro does not steal the oil.

He tries to contaminate the full container with a small black vial marked `G.O.M.`.

Do not fully explain what the black liquid is yet. Show enough for curiosity:

- the vial hisses when opened
- the liquid crawls instead of pours
- the FatHopes request screen glitches for one second
- Sparron's scanner later labels it: `foreign contaminant detected`
- Glinciro says, "Satu titik cukup."

The idea:

> Glinciro profits when clean resources become dirty problems.

## Character Jobs

### Kit

Kit owns the opening.

He is the hard worker who stops the pour and teaches the vendor the field rule.

Key line:

"JANGAN."

Practical line:

"Longkang bukan tempat buang minyak. Simpan dulu."

Episode job:

- stop the pour
- show the storage step
- later realise Glinciro is trying to make the saved oil useless
- physically protect the tong

### Sparron

Sparron proves the oil has value only if kept clean.

Key line:

"Kalau tak campur air, this can still be collected."

Episode job:

- scan the oil
- explain clean storage
- detect Glinciro's contaminant later
- give the audience the clear rule: clean oil has value, contaminated oil creates problems

### Dr.UP

Dr.UP should be calm, sharp, and not corny.

Key line:

"He did not want the oil. He wanted us to lose the oil."

Episode job:

- help translate the bigger point
- keep the vendor from feeling stupid
- frame G.O.M. as a real threat

### Glinciro

Glinciro is the first visible G.O.M. villain.

He does not appear until the oil has value.

Key entrance line:

"Banyak tu minyak."

Key sabotage line:

"Satu titik cukup."

Language:

70% Malay / 30% English controlled Manglish. He speaks in short, calm lines that hint he knows more than everyone else.

Episode job:

- reveal that G.O.M. understands the value of clean saved oil
- sabotage the oil instead of stealing it
- make the heroes realise they are fighting a syndicate, not random bad habits

### Vendor

The vendor is an older local Malay uncle. He speaks Bahasa Melayu only.

He is practical and believable.

Day 1:
He thinks oil is a disposal problem.

Another day:
He tries to do the right thing, but Glinciro turns his clean saved oil into a target.

His actual conflict:

"Saya dah ikut cara betul. Kenapa masih ada orang kacau?"

Episode 1 outcome:

The vendor does not fail. The attack proves that proper collection has enemies.

## Beat Sheet

### Beat 1: Almost Poured

Time:
0-6 seconds.

Visual:
Real warung/mamak back area. Vendor lifts a container of used cooking oil and tilts it toward the longkang.

Kit slides in and catches the container.

Kit:
"JANGAN."

Purpose:
Immediate hook. Kit owns the opening.

### Beat 2: Vendor Pushback

Time:
6-15 seconds.

Vendor:
"Apa jangan? Minyak lama je."

Kit:
"Lama bukan maksud dia sampah."

Vendor:
"Habis nak letak mana? Kedai saya kecil."

Purpose:
Make the vendor's problem practical.

### Beat 3: Save It Properly

Time:
15-35 seconds.

Sparron scans the oil.

Sparron:
"Still usable. No water contamination."

Vendor:
"Bahasa biasa boleh?"

Sparron:
"Minyak ni masih boleh collect."

Dr.UP helps Kit steady the container.

Dr.UP:
"It is only waste if you waste it."

Kit points to the proper container.

Kit:
"Simpan sini. Jangan campur air. Bila penuh, telefon FatHopes."

Vendor:
"Bila penuh baru telefon?"

Kit:
"Ya. Baru berbaloi jalan."

Purpose:
The vendor decides to save the oil. This is the first win.

### Beat 4: Time Passes

Time:
35-42 seconds.

Visual montage:

- oil goes into proper container
- lid closes
- day changes
- lunch rush
- more oil saved
- marker line on the container rises
- container becomes full

Text card or visual cue:
"Another day..."

Purpose:
Show the vendor actually follows through.

### Beat 5: Vendor Is About To Call FatHopes

Time:
42-52 seconds.

Visual:
Evening/closing time. The shop is quieter. The full container sits beside the vendor. He opens his phone and is about to call/request FatHopes.

Vendor:
"Penuh dah. Saya telefon dulu."

A voice from the dark:
"Banyak tu minyak."

Vendor freezes.

Purpose:
Set up Glinciro's entrance with tension.

### Beat 6: Glinciro Appears

Time:
52-65 seconds.

Visual:
Vendor looks into the dark side of the alley. A white hat appears first. Glinciro walks forward slowly, but stays half-hidden.

Glinciro:
"Uncle simpan elok. Bagus."

Vendor:
"Kau siapa?"

Glinciro:
"Orang yang tahu macam mana nak rosakkan value."

He takes out a tiny black vial marked `G.O.M.`

Purpose:
Introduce Glinciro as a saboteur, not a thief.

### Beat 7: The Sabotage

Time:
65-82 seconds.

Vendor:
"Jangan sentuh. Itu saya punya."

Glinciro:
"Saya tak ambil."

He opens the vial. The black liquid crawls at the mouth of the bottle.

Glinciro:
"Saya cuma kacau sikit."

Vendor:
"Kacau apa?"

Glinciro:
"Satu titik cukup."

Vendor:
"Kenapa kau buat macam ni?"

Glinciro:
"Sebab kalau uncle hilang percaya, minyak ni balik jadi masalah."

He flicks one drop toward the container.

Purpose:
Make Glinciro's threat more interesting: he ruins value rather than taking it.

### Beat 8: Heroes Interrupt

Time:
82-98 seconds.

Kit arrives and blocks the drop with the container lid.

Kit:
"Oi. Kacau kari boleh. Minyak jangan."

The black drop hits the lid and sizzles.

Sparron scans it.

Sparron:
"Foreign contaminant detected."

Vendor:
"Habis minyak saya?"

Sparron:
"Belum. Nasib baik kena lid."

Purpose:
Let the heroes win the physical beat while revealing a bigger enemy.

### Beat 9: Glinciro Reveals G.O.M.

Time:
98-112 seconds.

Glinciro smiles, not angry.

Glinciro:
"Tak apa. Hari ni satu tong selamat."

He taps the black vial.

Glinciro:
"Tapi Fataysia banyak tong."

Dr.UP:
"You are not attacking the oil."

Glinciro:
"Saya serang kepercayaan."

Dr.UP:
"G.O.M."

Vendor:
"Apa itu G.O.M.?"

Dr.UP:
"Orang yang untung bila kita buang benda bernilai."

Purpose:
Introduce G.O.M. without a lore dump.

### Beat 10: Glinciro Exit Hook

Time:
112-125 seconds.

Glinciro steps backward into the dark.

Glinciro:
"Teruskan ajar orang simpan minyak."

He smiles.

Glinciro:
"Senang saya tahu mana nak kacau."

He disappears.

Kit looks at the saved tong.

Kit:
"Okay. Now kena jaga tong also?"

Sparron:
"Correct."

Kit:
"Best. More kerja."

Purpose:
Set up the series threat and end on Kit's workload comedy.

## Visual Gags

- Kit catches the container just before the oil falls.
- Vendor uses a marker line on the container like a "level up" bar.
- Montage makes saved oil feel like progress, not trash.
- Glinciro's hat appears before his face in the dark.
- The black G.O.M. liquid crawls instead of pouring normally.
- Kit blocks the contaminant with a lid like a tiny shield.
- The lid sizzles dramatically from one tiny drop.
- Kit realises protecting the oil means more work for him.

## What Must Be Clear

- The vendor first learns to save the oil.
- Glinciro appears only when the oil has become valuable.
- Glinciro does not steal the oil.
- Glinciro is part of G.O.M.
- G.O.M. wants clean resources to become dirty problems.
- Glinciro's motive is to break vendor trust in proper collection.
- The heroes protect the oil and reveal the bigger syndicate threat.
- FatHopes is the proper collection path.

## What To Avoid

- Do not introduce Glinciro in the opening.
- Do not make Glinciro cartoonishly evil.
- Do not make the vendor responsible for the sabotage.
- Do not reveal the full G.O.M. plan yet.
- Do not explain SAF in detail.
- Do not make Dr.UP sound like a motivational speaker.
- Do not make the ending a corporate CTA.

## Final Episode Test

The episode is working if someone can retell it as:

> "A vendor almost pours oil into the longkang, Kit stops him, and the vendor starts saving it. Later, when the container is full and he is about to call FatHopes, Glinciro appears from G.O.M. and tries to contaminate the oil. The heroes stop him, but realise G.O.M. is trying to turn valuable resources back into pollution."
