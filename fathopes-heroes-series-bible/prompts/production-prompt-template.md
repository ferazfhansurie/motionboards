# Production Prompt Template

Use this when generating stills, storyboards, or short animated shots.

## Base Visual Prompt

Mixed-media animated comedy scene set in real Malaysia. Real photographed background with 2D comic/anime FatHopes Energy superhero characters composited into the scene. Clean bold outlines, expressive cartoon acting, practical real-world UCO collection details, FatHopes green/teal/black/white palette, grounded contact shadows, handheld documentary framing, funny-serious environmental action tone.

Do not copy any specific existing cartoon style. Use original mixed-reality animation with real Malaysian locations and 2D heroes.

## Character Consistency Block

Include the relevant character details:

- Dr.UP: tall confident public-facing hero, black hair, white long coat, deep green shirt, black pants, black boots and gloves, bold presenter energy.
- Sparron: blonde strategist girl, green translucent glasses/visor, black fitted top, green pants, gloves, boots, utility pouch, sharp technical energy.
- Kit: stocky hard-working field operator, teal jumpsuit, reflective silver bands, black gloves, black boots, dark work hat, grounded practical energy.
- Glinciro: hat-wearing villain, white hat with dark band, black vest, white shirt, black shorts, sandals, charming slippery troublemaker energy.

## Episode Shot Prompt Format

Scene:
[Where are we?]

Action:
[What is happening?]

Characters:
[Which heroes are present and what are their poses?]

Comedy Beat:
[What makes it funny?]

UCO Detail:
[Oil drum, drain, fryer, app, route, tanker, gloves, funnel, etc.]

Camera:
[Wide, close-up, handheld, low angle, reaction shot.]

Lighting:
[Natural daylight, fluorescent kitchen, rainy back alley, depot evening.]

Dialogue Overlay:
[Optional short text, max one sentence.]

Voice / Accent:
All character voices are Malaysian-accented English/Manglish (Klang Valley), even on full-English lines. Malay lines are Bahasa Melayu Malaysia, NOT Bahasa Indonesia/Jakarta: final "-a" is a schwa (saya = "sa-yuh", apa = "a-puh"), soft "r", relaxed KL intonation. No Indonesian accent, no neutral American accent, no British RP, no generic international voice. Dr.UP: confident Malaysian TV-presenter accent. Sparron: sharp KL young-professional accent. Kit: heavier BM-influenced, gruff. Glinciro: smooth, theatrical Malaysian-English. Vendors: real unpolished local accent.

## Character Uniqueness

Each character is a single, unique individual: exactly ONE Dr.UP, ONE Sparron, ONE Kit, ONE Glinciro, ONE of any vendor. Character reference images are identity locks, not instructions to place multiple copies in frame. Never clone, duplicate, mirror, or twin a character; never show two of the same person in the same shot, in the background, or in a reflection.

## Negative Prompt / Avoid

Avoid duplicate characters, cloned or twinned heroes, two of the same person in frame, background copies of a main character, mirrored duplicates in reflections, generic futuristic city, neon cyberpunk, copying existing cartoon shows, random capes unless already in character design, polished corporate explainer look, making vendors look foolish, unreadable text, changing character outfits, changing hair colors, extra random heroes.
