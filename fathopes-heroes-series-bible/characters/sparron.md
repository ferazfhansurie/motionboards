# Character Sheet: Sparron

## Role

The green-glasses strategist and tech-energy hero. Sparron understands data, contamination, oil quality, app flows, impact metrics, and the bigger sustainability chain.

## Visual Identity

- Blonde hair.
- Green translucent glasses/visor.
- Black fitted top.
- Green pants.
- Black gloves and boots.
- Utility pouch.
- Sharp, alert body language.

## Personality

- Smart and direct.
- Slightly impatient.
- Strategic.
- Sees patterns quickly.
- Loves proof, clean systems, and measurable impact.
- Tries to stay composed even when the team is chaotic.

## Comedy Engine

Sparron explains a simple problem with high-level technical seriousness, then Kit translates it into field language.

## Language

65% English / 35% Malay.

## Sample Lines

- "That is not oil storage. That is a future incident report."
- "If the oil is clean, conversion value goes up."
- "Guys, please stop smelling the sample."
- "This app flow is literally three taps."

## Weakness

Can over-explain and forget normal people are busy.

## Episode Function

Use Sparron when the story needs facts, analysis, an app explanation, a fast diagnosis, or the sustainability link.

## Long-Term Growth

Sparron starts by believing the right information should be enough to change behaviour.

Across the series, she learns that facts need empathy, timing, and trust. Her arc is becoming a strategist who understands people, not just systems.
