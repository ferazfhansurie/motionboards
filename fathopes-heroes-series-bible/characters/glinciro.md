# Character Sheet: Glinciro

## Role

The hat-wearing villain from Geng Orang Minyak (G.O.M.). Glinciro understands value before everyone else does, but he uses that knowledge to spread careless disposal, contaminate resources, and make communities lose trust in proper collection.

## Visual Identity

- White hat with dark band.
- Black vest.
- White shirt.
- Black shorts.
- Sandals.
- Confident grin.
- Smooth, slippery body language.
- Looks charming before he looks dangerous.

## Personality

- Polite.
- Patient.
- Precise.
- Quietly arrogant.
- Makes people feel late to a game he already understands.
- Treats other people's effort as something he can ruin if it benefits G.O.M.

## Motive

Glinciro works for G.O.M. because clean collection threatens their dirty economy.

When vendors save oil properly, FatHopes can trace it, collect it, and turn it into real value. That means less oil in drains, fewer desperate shortcuts, fewer contaminated batches, and less chaos for G.O.M. to exploit.

Glinciro's job is to break trust before the system works.

He does not always need to steal oil. If he can make a vendor think, "Ala, simpan elok pun kena kacau," then the vendor may stop caring and go back to dumping, mixing, or selling to shady collectors.

Core belief:

> "Kalau orang percaya pada sistem bersih, G.O.M. rugi."

## Comedy Engine

Glinciro does not need to shout. He makes one calm sentence land like a threat. The comedy comes from how refined he is while creating obvious mess.

## Language

70% Malay / 30% English, in controlled Manglish.

He speaks slowly and intentionally. He does not over-explain. He sounds local, refined, and dangerous, not like a salesman.

## Sample Lines

- "Uncle simpan elok. Bagus. Sayang kalau rosak."
- "Orang buang value hari-hari. G.O.M. cuma tolong lajukan."
- "Jangan risau, uncle. Benda ni bukan untuk uncle faham."
- "Cakap dekat FatHopes, satu tong bersih pun boleh jadi masalah."
- "Saya tak perlu ambil. Saya cuma perlu kacau sikit."
- "Satu vendor hilang percaya, sepuluh tong balik longkang."

## Weakness

He believes ruining a good system is easier than beating it fairly.

## Episode Function

Use Glinciro when the story needs sabotage, contamination, careless disposal, shortcuts, illegal practices, bad incentives, or a rival who makes pollution feel inevitable.

## Long-Term Growth

Glinciro starts as a G.O.M. saboteur: he profits from confusion, contamination, and careless disposal. His motive is not random evil. He is protecting a dirty economy from a clean system.

Over time, reveal that he respects value but hates systems because he thinks they leave small players behind. This gives him a possible redemption path later, but do not redeem him too quickly.
