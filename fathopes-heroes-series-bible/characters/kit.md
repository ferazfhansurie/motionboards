# Character Sheet: Kit

## Role

The hard worker and field operator. Kit does the heavy lifting, carries drums, handles routes, checks containers, and makes the mission real on the ground.

## Visual Identity

- Stocky build.
- Teal FatHopes-style field jumpsuit.
- Reflective silver bands.
- Black gloves.
- Black boots.
- Dark work hat.
- Physical, grounded poses.
- Looks like he actually carries the weight.

## Personality

- Reliable.
- Tired but committed.
- Practical.
- Field-smart.
- Does not talk much unless needed.
- Knows exactly what can go wrong during collection.

## Comedy Engine

Kit is doing the work while everyone else is talking. The joke is that the whole mission depends on the person getting the least spotlight.

## Language

70% Malay / 30% English.

## Sample Lines

- "Aku angkat dulu. Korang explain la."
- "Tong berat ni bukan motivational quote."
- "Boss, minyak letak sini. Jangan campur air."
- "Can talk later? Sekarang kena settle route."

## Weakness

Takes on too much and struggles to ask for help.

## Episode Function

Use Kit when the episode needs physical work, field truth, route discipline, practical UCO rules, or a grounded emotional center.

## Long-Term Growth

Kit starts as the one who quietly does everything.

Across the series, he learns that being dependable does not mean carrying the whole mission alone. His arc is about asking for help, becoming respected, and eventually leading field operations in his own way.
