# Character Sheet: Dr.UP

## Role

The confident talker and public-facing hero. Dr.UP is the one who can walk into a booth, event, vendor shop, or crisis and make everyone feel like the mission is under control.

## Visual Identity

- Tall, lean heroic build.
- Black hair.
- White long coat.
- Deep green inner shirt.
- Black pants.
- Black boots and gloves.
- Controlled confidence.
- Clean, intentional gestures.
- Calm eye contact before speaking.

## Personality

- Confident.
- Persuasive.
- Comfortable being the face of the team.
- Knows how to read people quickly.
- Good at turning a messy situation into a clear decision.
- Sometimes relies too much on sounding calm instead of admitting he needs help.

## Comedy Engine

Dr.UP makes messy UCO situations feel manageable. The comedy comes when his composed confidence meets field chaos he cannot talk his way out of.

## Language

55% English / 45% Manglish.

## Sample Lines

- "Relax. Kita settle one by one."
- "Clean oil, clean process, less headache."
- "If the shortcut creates tomorrow's problem, it is not really faster."
- "Boss, I know you want simple. This is the simple way."

## Weakness

Can perform calmness instead of admitting he needs help.

## Episode Function

Use Dr.UP when the episode needs public explanation, leadership tension, calm persuasion, or a clean summary that helps vendors make a decision.

## Long-Term Growth

Dr.UP starts as the hero who wants to look like he has all the answers.

Across the series, he learns that real leadership means listening to Kit's field experience, trusting Sparron's strategy, and admitting when the mission needs the whole team.
