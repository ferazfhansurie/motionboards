## 7 · SCENE / SHOT BREAKDOWN
**SC 1 — Reverse the tanker (EXT yard, day)**
- Boss guiding kid reversing the mini-tanker; AirPods misread → CLANG into a drum.
- Shots: wide (boss waving), OTS toward tanker, kid CU (AirPods, oblivious), the bump, boss eyes-closed reaction.

**SC 2 — Office confessional (INT, controlled)**
- Boss to camera: *"…SUMPAH aku tak faham dia."* → head down → up → *"Semalammm… aku fikir aku nampak hantu."*
- Shots: clean locked-off mid on boss + jerry can on desk dressing. **Clean audio critical (lav).**

**SC 3 — Night flashback (INT, dark)**
- Kid doing full TikTok dances alone among oil drums, phone-lit, CCTV/handheld grain look.
- Shots: handheld "found footage" of kid dancing; cut back to boss wide-eyed.

**SC 4 — Oil deposit + phone-call anxiety (EXT, day)**
- Kid pours oil (fine), boss: "Call Restoran Anjung confirm." Kid freezes → *"boss boleh tak kalau I text je?"* → *"I get bad anxiety from calls boss."*
- Shots: kid pouring (mid), 2-shot boss+kid, kid phone CU, **final boss stare into lens.**
