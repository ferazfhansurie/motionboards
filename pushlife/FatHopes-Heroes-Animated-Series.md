# FATHOPES HEROES - Animated Series Pack

Short-form animated comedy/action series for FatHopes Energy. Built around the existing character art in `public/Fathopes_heroes_animated/`.

## 1. Core Idea

Three overdramatic "eco heroes" solve very ordinary used-cooking-oil problems around Malaysia: kedai owners pouring oil into drains, restaurants hoarding smelly tins, depot chaos, and people not understanding that old oil can become real money and cleaner fuel.

The joke is scale mismatch:

- The mission is treated like a superhero anime.
- The actual problem is a leaky minyak masak tin behind a mamak.
- The brand truth stays real: collect UCO properly, pay vendors, prevent drain pollution, support sustainable aviation fuel.

Tone: fast, funny, Malaysian, slightly anime, not preachy. Think "Saturday morning hero squad meets KL kedai belakang."

## 3. Format

- Platform: TikTok, Reels, Shorts
- Ratio: 9:16
- Runtime: 20-35 seconds
- Language: 80% Bahasa Melayu, 20% English/Manglish
- Structure:
  1. Cold open problem, 0-3s
  2. Hero entrance, 3-7s
  3. Silly obstacle or misunderstanding, 7-18s
  4. Simple UCO truth, 18-25s
  5. Punchline + soft brand close, 25-35s

Important: no long explainers. Every episode should make one point only.

## 4. Visual Rules

- Keep the existing anime line-art character style.
- Use FatHopes dark green, white, black, and lime accents.
- Backgrounds can be semi-real Malaysian locations: mamak back lane, food court, depot, pasar malam, mall roadshow booth, petrol-station-style collection point.
- Characters should stay recognizable from the supplied PNGs. Use the reference images as strict identity/style references.
- Use dynamic comic panels, speed lines, impact frames, and quick reaction cuts.
- Avoid generic forest/earth-globe imagery unless used as a joke.

## 6. Recurring Locations

- Mamak back lane with stacked oil tins
- Food court loading bay
- FatHopes depot with drums, mini-tanker, weighing scale
- Pasar malam stall after closing
- Mall roadshow booth
- Apartment surau/community collection day
- Restaurant kitchen doorway, never inside unsafe hot-oil zones unless staged safely

## 7. Episode Engine

Every episode should answer one of these:

- Why should I not pour oil into the drain?
- How do I earn from used cooking oil?
- What happens after FatHopes collects it?
- Why must used oil be stored properly?
- What is SAF in one simple joke?
- Why is clean documentation important for businesses?
- Why does FatHopes care about weight, records, and routes?

## 8. Season 1 Episode List

### EP01 - Jangan Masuk Longkang

Point: Do not pour used oil into drains.

Cold open: A kedai worker lifts a pot toward the drain. Anime alarm siren. D-R Up appears like he is stopping a meteor.

Beat:
- Worker: "Ala sikit je."
- Kit sniffs the air from across the street: "Sikit? Longkang tu dah trauma."
- Sparron slides in with a clean oil tin.
- They redirect the oil into proper storage.

Punchline:
- D-R Up gives a heroic speech.
- Worker says, "So... boleh dapat duit?"
- All three freeze. Kit: "Itu part paling penting, baru kau tanya."

Close text:
"Minyak terpakai jangan buang. Simpan. Jual."

### EP02 - Tin Belakang Kedai

Point: Proper storage makes collection easier and cleaner.

Cold open: A leaking oil tin creates a dramatic "boss fight" puddle.

Beat:
- Kit treats the leaking tin like an enemy.
- Sparron wants to film it but slips slightly.
- D-R Up explains: closed container, no water mixed in, label/store properly.

Punchline:
- Vendor says, "Saya ingat tong ni hiasan belakang kedai."
- Kit: "Bang, ini bukan interior design."

Close text:
"Tutup elok. Simpan bersih. Senang collect."

### EP03 - Minyak Lama, Duit Baru

Point: Used oil has value.

Cold open: Vendor nearly throws old oil away because "dah tak guna."

Beat:
- Sparron transforms the phrase "dah tak guna" into a comic shockwave.
- D-R Up shows a tiny animated value chain: kedai -> FatHopes -> processed -> SAF.
- Kit quietly points at payment.

Punchline:
- Vendor: "Jadi benda yang busuk ni boleh bayar kopi saya?"
- Kit: "Kalau kau simpan elok, kopi pun boleh, roti canai pun boleh."

Close text:
"Dulu buang. Sekarang boleh jadi pendapatan."

### EP04 - Route Sacred

Point: Collection routes and records matter.

Cold open: Sparron says, "Kita wing it je route hari ni."

Beat:
- Kit goes silent.
- Anime thunder.
- D-R Up opens a route map like a battle plan.
- One missed kedai causes a vendor to call: "Minyak saya penuh dah ni!"

Punchline:
- Kit whispers, "Route bukan vibe."

Close text:
"Collection kena konsisten."

### EP05 - SAF? Macam Flight Tu?

Point: Simple SAF awareness.

Cold open: Kid asks if used oil becomes "minyak goreng premium."

Beat:
- D-R Up nearly faints.
- Quick stylized chain: used cooking oil -> proper collection -> processing -> sustainable aviation fuel.
- Keep it simple, no technical lecture.

Punchline:
- Vendor looks at airplane graphic: "So minyak ayam goreng aku pergi holiday dulu dari aku?"
- Sparron: "At least dia ada passport."

Close text:
"Used cooking oil can support cleaner fuel."

### EP06 - Booth Panic

Point: Community collection days.

Cold open: Mall booth overwhelmed by aunties bringing bottles.

Beat:
- Sparron handles crowd energy.
- D-R Up labels bottles.
- Kit argues with one bottle that has sambal mixed inside.

Punchline:
- Aunty says, "Minyak ni dari raya lepas."
- Kit: "Saya boleh bau sejarah dia."

Close text:
"Bawa minyak terpakai. Kami timbang dan uruskan."
