# PUSH collector ID carousel — mural-first rebuild

## Shared world

One continuous night-time FatHopes collection depot, viewed while the camera tracks left-to-right along a single lime-green **verification route**. The route is a physical illuminated cable/light trail on the floor and walls—not seven unrelated arrows.

Slides 2–7 use the exact same depot world: black steel walls, green mini-tanker bay, overhead industrial lighting, lime safety rails, soft teal haze and a lime route. The route is always a single thick cable with one moving scan glow, at the same perceived height and scale on both sides of every panel.

Slide 1 deliberately starts before the depot: a deep forest-green field with one oversized, faint, authentic FatHopes Energy logo as its only background graphic. The lime route emerges from the logo mark, enters the lower-right edge, and carries the viewer into the real depot on Slide 2.

## Fixed hand-off map

| Boundary | Exit from left panel | Entry to right panel | Visual hand-off |

## Panel sequence

1. **Identify PUSH collector** — logo-only background, anonymous `NOT PUSH` reference inset, route begins.
2. **Step 1: Uniform** — real official polo front; route scans chest logo.
3. **Step 1: Uniform back** — the same collector turns away; route runs behind shoulder.
4. **Step 2: Vendor App** — route powers the real app / QR verification scan.
5. **Step 3: Mini tanker** — route becomes the tanker’s wheel-light trail.
6. **Step 4: Get verified** — route enters QR scanner and turns green only after verification.
7. **Case closed** — route resolves into four proof points and one clear final action.

## Generation rules

- Generate with Nano Banana 2 (`gemini-3.1-flash-image-preview`), one 4:5 panel at a time.
- Every prompt repeats the fixed incoming and outgoing edge coordinates above.
- Use real asset references only for uniform, app and mini-tanker details.
- No independent arrows, frames, floating cards or reset backgrounds.
- Assess every generated panel as a downscaled left-to-right mural before accepting it; regenerate only the panels whose route, horizon, lighting or subject scale break the hand-off.
